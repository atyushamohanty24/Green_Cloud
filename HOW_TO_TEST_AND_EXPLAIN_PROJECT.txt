PART 1: PROJECT OVERVIEW IN SIMPLE WORDS (UNDERSTAND IN 2 MINUTES)
================================================================================

1.1 WHAT IS GREENCLOUD AI?
--------------------------
GreenCloud AI is an intelligent software platform that connects to cloud providers
like Amazon Web Services (AWS). It monitors servers and databases, uses Machine
Learning to predict how much computing power they will actually need in the next
24 hours, and automatically downsizes or turns off wasted resources.

At the same time, it tracks the global carbon footprint of the electricity grids
powering those data centers, and helps schedule heavy computing jobs in regions
powered by clean, renewable energy (wind, solar, and hydro).

1.2 THE EVERYDAY ANALOGY (THE SMART THERMOSTAT FOR CLOUD)
---------------------------------------------------------
Think about the air conditioning in an office building:
- BAD WAY: Leaving all AC units running at maximum 16°C 24 hours a day, 7 days
  a week, even when nobody is in the office at night or on weekends.
  -> Massive electricity bill, huge carbon footprint.
- GREENCLOUD WAY: A smart AI thermostat that learns when employees arrive and
  leave, predicts when the room will be hot, automatically cools the rooms when
  needed, lowers power when empty, and switches to solar power when available.

In our project:
- The AC units are AWS cloud servers (EC2 instances, databases, and storage).
- The smart thermostat is GreenCloud AI's Machine Learning engine.

1.3 THE CORE PROBLEM WE SOLVE (FINOPS + GREENOPS)
-------------------------------------------------
1. Cloud Over-provisioning: Software developers are afraid their websites will crash
   during traffic surges. To be safe, they buy huge 8-core CPU servers for apps that
   only need 1 or 2 cores. Over 50% of rented cloud hardware sits idle doing nothing!
2. Financial Waste (FinOps): Companies spend millions of dollars every year on
   unused virtual machines.
3. Environmental Damage (GreenOps): Data centers worldwide produce over 2% of global
   greenhouse gases (equal to the entire commercial aviation industry). Running idle
   servers directly burns coal and gas in power plants.
4. Fear of Manual Changes: Engineers rarely downsize servers because manual resizing
   might cause downtime. GreenCloud AI provides automated safety checks and instant
   one-click rollback to eliminate this fear.

PART 2: WHAT DATA DOES THIS PROJECT TAKE IN & GIVE OUT? (DATA DICTIONARY)
================================================================================

2.1 INPUT DATA (WHAT THE SYSTEM INGESTS)
---------------------------------------
GreenCloud AI ingests 4 primary categories of data:

A. CLOUD TELEMETRY METRICS (Sampled Hourly from AWS CloudWatch):
   - `resource_id`: Unique identifier (e.g., `i-0aebc7c0d53fa1c09`, `prod-postgresql-primary`)
   - `timestamp`: Date and time of metric capture (UTC)
   - `cpu_utilization`: Percentage of CPU used (0.0% to 100.0%)
   - `memory_utilization`: Percentage of RAM used (0.0% to 100.0%)
   - `network_in_kb` / `network_out_kb`: Network bandwidth traffic transferred
   - `request_count`: Total HTTP incoming user requests handled per hour
   - `storage_used_gb`: Current disk or bucket storage consumed in Gigabytes

B. CLOUD RESOURCE INVENTORY & PRICING:
   - `service_type`: EC2 (virtual machine), RDS (database), S3 (storage), Lambda (serverless)
   - `instance_type`: Current hardware tier (e.g., `t3.xlarge`, `c5.2xlarge`, `Standard`)
   - `hourly_cost`: Official AWS On-Demand billing rate ($/hour)
   - `base_carbon_hourly`: Hardware operational carbon intensity (grams of CO2e / hour)

C. REGIONAL ELECTRICITY GRID CARBON DATA:
   - `region`: Cloud data center region (e.g., `us-east-1`, `eu-west-1`, `ap-south-1`)
   - `carbon_intensity_g_co2_kwh`: Grams of CO2 emitted per kilowatt-hour of power
     * Example: us-east-1 (Virginia, USA) = 375 g CO2/kWh (moderate fossil fuels)
     * Example: eu-west-1 (Ireland)       = 180 g CO2/kWh (78% wind/hydro renewable)
     * Example: ap-southeast-1 (Singapore)= 410 g CO2/kWh (natural gas)
   - `grid_cleanliness_pct`: Percentage of clean/renewable energy on the local grid

D. BATCH JOB SPECIFICATIONS (From DevOps Engineers for Scheduling):
   - `job_name`: e.g., "AI Training Batch", "Weekly Data Analytics"
   - `duration_hours`: Estimated execution runtime (e.g., 6 hours)
   - `required_vcpu` & `required_ram_gb`: Hardware capacity required
   - `preferred_regions`: Candidate deployment regions to evaluate

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2 OUTPUT DATA & GENERATED METRICS (WHAT THE SYSTEM PRODUCES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GreenCloud AI generates 5 concrete, actionable outputs:

1. THE COMPOSITE GREEN SCORE (0 to 100):
   - An executive sustainability metric summarizing the overall health of the
     cloud infrastructure. Higher is cleaner, cheaper, and safer.
   - Sub-score breakdowns:
     * Utilization Efficiency % (Are servers running at healthy 40-70% CPU instead of idle <15%?)
     * Cost Efficiency % (Ratio of actual cost vs optimal rightsized cost)
     * Carbon Efficiency % (Proportion of compute hosted on clean energy grids)
     * Safety & SLA Score % (100% when no performance regressions or rollbacks occur)

2. 24-HOUR WORKLOAD DEMAND PREDICTIONS:
   - `forecasted_cpu_24h`: Predicted average CPU for the next 24 hours (e.g., 10.9%)
   - `predicted_demand`: Categorical demand level: "Low", "Medium", "High", "Critical"
   - `confidence_score`: Machine Learning model confidence percentage (e.g., 95.2%)
   - Model accuracy metrics: Accuracy = 95%, F1 Score = 0.94, MAE = 1.2%

3. CONCRETE OPTIMIZATION RECOMMENDATIONS:
   - `id`: Recommendation ID (e.g., Rec #1, Rec #2)
   - `type`: "Rightsize", "Stop Idle", "Storage Lifecycle", "Scale Down"
   - `title`: e.g., "Downsize Over-provisioned EC2 Instance"
   - `current_spec` vs `target_spec`: e.g., `t3.xlarge` -> `t3.medium`
   - `estimated_savings_monthly`: Monthly dollars saved (e.g., $89.86 / month)
   - `estimated_carbon_reduction_monthly`: Monthly CO2 eliminated (e.g., 20.0 kg CO2 / month)
   - `risk_level`: "Low Risk" (Safe to automate) or "High Risk" (Requires human admin sign-off)
   - `status`: "Pending", "Approved", "Executed", "Rolled Back"

4. CARBON-AWARE SCHEDULER RECOMMENDATIONS:
   - `recommended_region`: Cleanest cloud region (e.g., `eu-west-1`)
   - `recommended_start_time`: Optimal time window (e.g., 11:00 UTC - 17:00 UTC)
   - `carbon_emissions_saved_pct`: Emissions reduction percentage (e.g., 56.1% saved)
   - `explanation`: Detailed plain-language justification of why this region was picked.

5. SECURITY AUDIT TRAIL & ROLLBACK HISTORY:
   - `event_type`: "ACTION_EXECUTED", "ROLLBACK_TRIGGERED", "SYSTEM_INITIALIZED"
   - `actor`: IAM role that triggered the action (e.g., `IAM:ADMIN`, `SYSTEM_AUTO_VERIFIER`)
   - `before_snapshot`: Server state, CPU, and cost BEFORE modification
   - `after_snapshot`: Server state and AWS API response AFTER modification
   - `rollback_reason`: Root cause explanation if a regression was detected.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3 THE GREEN SCORE FORMULA EXPLAINED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The overall Green Score is calculated as a weighted average:

   Green Score = (0.30 * Utilization Score)
               + (0.35 * Cost Score)
               + (0.25 * Carbon Score)
               + (0.10 * Safety Score)

- Weight 0.30: Reward keeping CPU between 40% and 80% (penalize waste below 15%).
- Weight 0.35: Reward reducing unnecessary dollar spend.
- Weight 0.25: Reward placing workloads in low-carbon geographic regions.
- Weight 0.10: Reward maintaining zero SLA downtime and reliable automated actions.

3.2 THE MACHINE LEARNING FORECASTING ENGINE
------------------------------------------
- Algorithm: Random Forest Regressor & Random Forest Classifier (trained via Scikit-Learn).
- Input Features Extracted:
  1. `hour_of_day` (0 to 23): Captures daily diurnal peaks and nighttime dips.
  2. `day_of_week` (0 to 6): Captures weekday work traffic vs weekend quiet periods.
  3. `cpu_utilization` & `memory_utilization`: Current real-time load.
  4. `cpu_rolling_6h_mean`: Short-term moving average.
  5. `cpu_rolling_24h_mean`: Full-day moving average.
  6. `cpu_rolling_24h_max`: Peak workload spike in the last 24 hours.
- Prediction Output:
  Tells the optimizer: "This server is only going to use 12% CPU tomorrow with a peak
  of 18%. It does NOT need a 4-core t3.xlarge instance! A 2-core t3.medium is 100% safe."

3.3 THE OPTIMIZATION RULE ENGINE
--------------------------------
The Optimizer scans every resource against 4 core rules:
- Rule 1 (EC2 Rightsizing): If average CPU < 15% over 7 days, downsize instance
  (e.g., `t3.xlarge` -> `t3.medium`, saving $89.86/mo).
- Rule 2 (Compute Node Rightsizing): If average CPU < 12% on compute clusters,
  downsize (e.g., `c5.2xlarge` -> `c5.large`, saving $183.60/mo).
- Rule 3 (Stop Idle Instances): If average CPU < 3% with zero network traffic,
  stop the instance (saving 100% of compute spend: $29.95/mo).
- Rule 4 (S3 Glacier Lifecycle): If an S3 storage bucket has had zero read access
  for 21+ days, apply an automated lifecycle rule to Glacier Instant Retrieval
  (cuts storage cost by 82% from $0.023/GB to $0.004/GB).
- Rule 5 (Lambda Concurrency Scale Down): If provisioned concurrency is 50 but
  average executions are only 3, downsize concurrency to 10.

3.4 THE SAFETY RULES & RISK GOVERNANCE ENGINE
---------------------------------------------
To prevent accidental outages:
- Low-Risk Action:
  * Savings < $200/month AND capacity reduction < 50%.
  * Safe for standard operators to execute.
- High-Risk Action:
  * Savings > $200/month OR reducing capacity by > 50%.
  * Requires explicit approval from a user with the `admin` IAM role.

3.5 REAL AWS CLOUD EXECUTION (BOTO3 INTEGRATION)
------------------------------------------------
GreenCloud AI communicates directly with live AWS APIs using Python Boto3:
- EC2 API: Calls `ec2.describe_instances()`, `ec2.stop_instances()`, and
  `ec2.modify_instance_attribute(InstanceType={'Value': new_spec})`.
- S3 API: Calls `s3.put_bucket_lifecycle_configuration()` to apply archival rules.
- CloudWatch API: Ingests `cloudwatch.get_metric_data()` and manages alarms.
- Fallback Mode: If running offline or without credentials, it uses an internal
  simulation provider so development and demos never fail.

3.6 AUTOMATED REGRESSION VERIFIER & ROLLBACK
--------------------------------------------
Immediately before making any change:
1. BEFORE SNAPSHOT: The system records CPU, RAM, instance spec, cost, and timestamp.
2. ACTION EXECUTION: The server is resized or modified in AWS.
3. VERIFICATION CHECK: The system observes performance post-action. If errors or
   latency violate the SLA, `rollback_action()` is called automatically.
4. RESTORATION: The baseline hardware spec is restored immediately, and a
   "ROLLBACK_TRIGGERED" event is logged to the immutable audit trail.

3.7 CARBON-AWARE MULTI-REGION SCHEDULER
---------------------------------------
For heavy computing tasks (like AI training jobs, batch rendering, or backups):
1. Ingests real regional carbon grid intensity (g CO2/kWh).
2. Calculates the carbon emissions for running the job in Region A vs Region B.
3. Automatically recommends the cleanest region (e.g., Ireland with 78% renewables)
   and the optimal hours of the day (when wind/solar power peaks).

4.2 PLAIN ENGLISH GLOSSARY (TECHNICAL TERMS SIMPLIFIED)
-------------------------------------------------------
- Rightsizing: Changing an oversized server (e.g. 16GB RAM) to a smaller, cheaper
  size (e.g. 4GB RAM) that matches real traffic.
- Over-provisioning: Buying more computer hardware than you actually need.
- Carbon Intensity (g CO2/kWh): How much pollution a power plant generates to make
  one unit of electricity. Clean solar/wind = low carbon; coal/gas = high carbon.
- Boto3: The official Python library used to control Amazon Web Services (AWS) via code.
- S3 Glacier Instant Retrieval: An ultra-cheap AWS storage tier designed for files
  rarely accessed (like old logs), saving 82% on storage bills.
- Closed-Loop Automation: A system that monitors, decides, executes, and verifies
  changes on its own without needing manual intervention for every small step.
- Rollback: Restoring a server back to its previous state if a new change causes issues.

PART 6: TOP 10 VIVA & TEACHER QUESTIONS WITH WINNING ANSWERS
================================================================================

Q1: What is the main innovation of GreenCloud AI?
A1: "Most tools only look at dollars (FinOps). GreenCloud AI couples financial cost
     optimization with regional electricity grid carbon intensity (GreenOps).
     It uses Machine Learning to predict workload demand 24 hours ahead, safely
     downsizes servers, shifts batch jobs to clean energy regions, and provides
     automated regression rollback."

Q2: What is the difference between FinOps and GreenOps?
A2: "FinOps focuses strictly on minimizing financial cloud spend (dollars saved).
     GreenOps focuses on reducing the environmental impact (kilograms of CO2e avoided).
     GreenCloud AI combines both: rightsizing saves both money AND electricity."

Q3: Why not just use AWS Compute Optimizer?
A3: "AWS Compute Optimizer only looks at cost and raw metrics. It does not evaluate
     regional carbon grid intensity (g CO2/kWh), does not support multi-region
     carbon-aware workload shifting, does not calculate a unified Green Score,
     and does not offer built-in automated regression rollback."

Q4: How do you calculate the Green Score?
A4: "It is a composite 0-100 index built on 4 weighted pillars:
     Utilization Efficiency (30%), Cost Efficiency (35%), Carbon Grid Efficiency (25%),
     and Safety & SLA Compliance (10%)."

Q5: Why did you choose Random Forest over a Deep Neural Network?
A5: "For tabular timeseries metrics (CPU, RAM, rolling averages), Random Forest and
     XGBoost achieve state-of-the-art accuracy, train in seconds without expensive GPUs
     (aligning with Green AI principles), and offer explainable feature importance."

Q6: What happens if an automated resize slows down our production application?
A6: "Step 10 of our pipeline is the Automated Verifier. If latency or error rates
     exceed our SLA threshold post-action, the system automatically triggers a rollback,
     restoring the original server specification and logging the event in the audit trail."

Q7: How does the Carbon-Aware Scheduler work?
A7: "It takes job parameters (duration, required vCPU, RAM) and queries regional grid
     carbon intensities. It scores regions based on renewable percentage and current
     carbon intensity, recommending the optimal execution window in the cleanest region."

Q8: What is least-privilege IAM and how is it used here?
A8: "Instead of granting full AdministratorAccess, we created a custom IAM policy
     (`GreenCloudLeastPrivilegeOptimizationPolicy`) strictly restricted to CloudWatch
     read metrics, EC2 stop/modify attributes, S3 lifecycle, and Lambda configuration."

Q9: What is S3 Glacier Instant Retrieval and why recommend it?
A9: "Standard S3 costs $0.023/GB/month. Glacier Instant Retrieval costs $0.004/GB/month
     (an 82% cost reduction) with millisecond retrieval. For logs rarely accessed after
     30 days, this saves huge amounts of storage hardware energy and cost."

Q10: What are the key takeaways from your project?
A10: "We proved that cloud sustainability and financial savings go hand-in-hand.
      By using predictive ML demand modeling combined with carbon grid awareness,
      we demonstrated a ~40% reduction in cloud compute costs and a ~50% reduction
      in carbon emissions, all while maintaining enterprise security and uptime SLA."
